# 📊 Telco Customer Churn Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for telecommunications companies, as losing customers can directly impact revenue and business growth.

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn patterns using **Power BI** to identify key factors influencing customer attrition. The dashboard provides insights into customer demographics, service subscriptions, contract preferences, billing patterns, and churn behavior.

The objective is to help businesses understand why customers leave and identify areas where customer retention strategies can be improved.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the overall customer churn rate.
- Understand churn patterns across customer demographics.
- Evaluate the impact of subscribed services on customer retention.
- Analyze churn based on contract types and payment methods.
- Identify customer segments with higher churn tendencies.
- Generate actionable insights to support customer retention strategies.

## 📂 Dataset Information

- **Dataset:** Telco Customer Churn
- **Source:** Telco Customer Churn Dataset
- **Total Records:** 7,043 customers
- **Original Columns:** 21
- **Domain:** Telecommunications

The dataset contains customer information, subscribed services, account details, billing information, tenure, and churn status.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and calculated columns
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Dataset handling

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation

The following data preparation steps were performed:

- Checked for missing values and inconsistencies.
- Handled data types and formatting issues.
- Standardized categorical values where required.
- Created calculated columns for better analysis.
- Grouped customer tenure into meaningful tenure segments.
- Created monthly charges bands to analyze customer spending patterns.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The overall customer churn rate is approximately **26.54%**.
- Customers with month-to-month contracts contribute significantly to churn.
- Fiber optic internet customers show comparatively higher churn.
- Customers using electronic check payments account for the largest share of churned customers.
- Senior citizens and customers with shorter tenure demonstrate higher churn tendencies.
- Contract type, tenure, internet service and payment method are important factors to investigate when understanding customer attrition.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Introduce customer retention offers for month-to-month contract customers.
- Improve customer onboarding and engagement during the initial months.
- Investigate service-related issues affecting fiber optic customers.
- Encourage convenient and reliable payment methods.
- Develop targeted retention campaigns for customer segments with higher churn rates.
